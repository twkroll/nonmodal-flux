### Neuro restriction

The full-state instantaneous pathway matrix `Q` is indefinite, but on the frozen two-dimensional admissible preparation space the cumulative minimum eigenvalue remains positive over the horizon ladder. Therefore this pilot does **not** demonstrate experimentally reachable positive and negative cumulative pathway extrema.

The correct claim is objective nonredundancy between terminal synaptic-filter storage and the positive cumulative contribution of a pre-defined physiological pathway on a physically admissible preparation space.

Also, `G_M` in Neuro is terminal synaptic-filter storage per fixed experimental input cost. It must not be called brain energy, metabolic energy, thermodynamic energy, or interpreted as an initial-storage gain unless that normalization is separately defined.

## 7. What is branch-independent and what is not

### Branch-independent framework layer

The transferable analysis tuple remains

\[
\mathfrak C=(A,M,Q,B,R_{\rm in}),
\]

with the common finite-time operators

\[
K_M(T)=R_{\rm in}^{-1/2}B^\dagger e^{A^\dagger T}Me^{AT}BR_{\rm in}^{-1/2},
\]

\[
K_Q(T)=R_{\rm in}^{-1/2}B^\dagger\left[\int_0^T e^{A^\dagger t}Qe^{At}\,dt\right]BR_{\rm in}^{-1/2}.
\]

The common diagnostic layer includes signed extrema, optimizer/subspace comparison, dimensionless gap, direct physical diagnostics and preregistered robustness checks.

### Branch-dependent semantics

The meanings of `M`, `Q`, `B`, and even the interpretation of `G_M` are domain specific and must never be flattened into one physical story.

In particular:

- Plasma: `M` is free energy and `Q` is signed particle flux;
- Climate: `M` is QG perturbation energy and `Q` is signed eddy heat flux;
- Neuro: `M` is synaptic-filter storage and `Q` is a signed pathway contribution to its rate; `R_in` is experimental pulse cost.
