### Discussion-level claim

The evidence supports three distinct methodological lessons:

1. positive storage/state optima can be poor proxies for a separately defined physical channel in some systems and admissible geometries;
2. large optimizer/subspace angles can coexist with small target-performance penalties;
3. large fixed-resolution objective separation can fail refinement and must not be promoted without robustness.

The third point is a methodological interpretation of the frozen Climate-B failure, not a general atmospheric theorem.
